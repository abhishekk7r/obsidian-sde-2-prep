> [!tip] ECS "Next Gen": answer key
> - Not an official AWS product name. Most likely means the **new ECS options from 2025–26**
> - **ECS Managed Instances** (Sep 2025): give vCPU / memory / arch, AWS picks, patches and runs the EC2 instances in your account. Middle ground between Fargate (no servers, less control) and EC2 launch type (full control, you patch)
> - **ECS Express Mode** (Nov 2025): give a container image, ECS creates the service, load balancer, scaling and a public URL in one step
> - **Built-in blue/green, canary, linear deploys** in ECS itself (no CodeDeploy needed)
> - If unsure in an interview: say "Do you mean Managed Instances / Express Mode?" and compare against Fargate vs EC2
