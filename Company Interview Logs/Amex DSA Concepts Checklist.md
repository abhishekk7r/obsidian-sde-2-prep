# Amex DSA: concept checklist

> [!note] Method per concept
> 1. Cold: when does this pattern apply, what is the template
> 2. Fresh variation, not a memorized problem (pseudocode, hints only)
> 3. Quick recall of own solved problems
> Tick only after a cold correct answer. Hard problems skipped.

## Amex-priority concepts
- [ ] 1 Sliding window (25 min): LSWRC, Max Consecutive Ones III, LRCR, Permutation in String. Fresh: Fruit Into Baskets
- [ ] 2 Two pointers, hashing, prefix sum (25 min): 3Sum, Two Sum, Closest Pair, Group Anagram, Longest Consecutive, Product of Array. Fresh: Subarray Sum Equals K
- [ ] 3 Stack and monotonic stack (20 min): Min Stack, Daily Temperatures, Decode String, Asteroid Collision. Fresh: Celebrity problem, Valid Parentheses
- [ ] 4 Linked list (25 min): Reverse, LRU Cache. Fresh: Cycle I and II, Merge Two Sorted Lists
- [ ] 5 Backtracking (20 min): Subsets, Word Search. Fresh: Combination Sum, Permutations
- [ ] 6 Trees (25 min): Invert, Max Depth, Diameter, LCA, Balanced, Good Nodes, Subtree. Fresh: Level Order, tree infection spread time
- [ ] 7 Intervals and sorting (15 min): Merge Intervals. Fresh: merge sort
- [ ] 8 Heap and top K (15 min): Top K Frequent, Bricks and Ladders. Fresh: Merge k Sorted Lists (idea only)

## Common patterns and weak spots
- [ ] 9 Grid and graph BFS/DFS (30 min): Number of Islands, Max Area, Clone Graph, Rotting Oranges, Walls and Gates, Pacific Atlantic, Surrounded Regions, Shortest Bridge
- [ ] 10 Topological sort (15 min): Course Schedule I and II (edge direction bug from Lenskart)
- [ ] 11 Binary search (15 min): Koko, Aggressive Cows. Fresh: Search in Rotated Sorted Array
- [ ] 12 DP (40 min): House Robber I/II/III, Min Cost Tickets, LCS. Fresh: Coin Change, Unique Paths, Word Break
- [ ] 13 Greedy and Kadane (10 min): Best Time to Buy and Sell, Max Subarray, Max Product Subarray

## Skipped (hard or unlikely)
Trapping Rain Water, Minimum Window Substring, First Missing Positive, Median of Two Sorted Arrays, Alien Dictionary, Cherry Pickup II, Edit Distance, Spread Stones, KMP and similar algorithm templates.
